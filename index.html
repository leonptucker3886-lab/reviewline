How it works

Set your Google review link once. After that, every finished job runs the funnel without you touching anything.

1 · Job done, ask sent

The moment you close a job, your customer gets a short, friendly review request by email or text — from you, not from a robot.

2 · Happy? Off to Google

Customers who had a good experience tap through straight to your Google review page. That's where five stars come from.

3 · Unhappy? You hear it first

Had a bad one? They land on a private feedback form instead — you get the chance to fix it before it becomes a one-star public post.

Does asking for reviews actually work?

Most happy customers don't review — not because they don't want to, but because nobody asks at the right moment. An automatic ask right after a good job is the single cheapest marketing a small business can do.

Is the "unhappy goes private" thing ethical?

Yes — we never block or filter reviews. Customers can always go straight to Google if they want. We just make sure you hear about problems while you can still fix them. That's called service.
